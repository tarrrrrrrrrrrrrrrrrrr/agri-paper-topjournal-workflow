# Citation Workflow

Use this module when adding, checking or revising references in an agricultural manuscript.

## Claim-to-citation workflow

1. Split manuscript text into citable claims.
2. Mark each claim type: background, method, interpretation, comparison, limitation or policy.
3. Search with `nature-citation` when the user requests Nature/CNS-style support; otherwise use current primary literature and official method sources.
4. Verify title, journal, year, volume, pages/article number, DOI and claim fit.
5. Insert citations only where they support the exact sentence.
6. Export reference-manager-ready records when requested.

## What needs citation

- Crop, quality or stress background claims.
- Known physiological or metabolic mechanisms.
- Established measurement methods.
- Statistical or modeling methods that are not standard in the field.
- Journal policy statements and data availability requirements.
- Claims comparing the present study with previous work.

## What does not need citation

- Results generated by the current experiment.
- Basic descriptions of the study design.
- A claim that is already directly supported by a figure or table from the manuscript.

## Reference quality rules

- Prefer peer-reviewed primary literature for mechanistic and agronomic claims.
- Prefer official manuals or standards for methods, instruments and repository policies.
- Avoid citing reviews for highly specific experimental claims unless they are used as background.
- Do not cite a paper only because its title contains similar keywords.
- Do not fabricate DOI, page range, article number or author list.

## Citation QA

Before submission:

- Every citation in text appears in the reference list.
- Every reference-list item is cited in text.
- Author-year or numbered style matches the target journal.
- Claims with strong causal language have direct experimental support.
- Literature comparisons do not contradict the current manuscript's design or sample size.
